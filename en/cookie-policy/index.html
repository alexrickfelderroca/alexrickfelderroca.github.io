Cookie policy

Last updated: July 2026.

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device when you visit. Cookies make the site work, remember your preferences and measure how the site is used. We also use equivalent technologies such as the browser's local storage.

Who uses cookies

The data controller is Reforma BCN ([PENDING: legal name and tax ID]). Some cookies are managed by third parties, mainly Google, through Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads.

Cookies we use

We group cookies into three categories. Except for the necessary ones, none are activated without your consent.

  • Necessary

    Always on

    Essential for the site to work and to remember your cookie decision. They require no consent and can't be turned off.

  • Analytics

    Let us measure in aggregate how the site is used (page views, traffic source) to improve it, via Google Analytics 4. They are only activated if you accept them.

  • Marketing

    Used to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and show relevant advertising via Google Ads. They are only activated if you accept them.

How we manage your consent

We use Google Consent Mode v2: until you accept, all non-essential cookies stay blocked. You can accept, reject or choose by category in the notice shown when you arrive.

Change or withdraw your consent

You can change your choice at any time, as easily as you gave it. Your decision is remembered for up to 180 days; after that we'll ask you again.

You can also block or delete cookies from your browser settings. Note that disabling some may affect how the site works.

More information

To learn how we process your personal data, see our privacy policy. If you have questions, write to us at info@reformabcn.es.
