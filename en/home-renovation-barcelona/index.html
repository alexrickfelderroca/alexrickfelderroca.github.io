Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to renovate a home in Barcelona?

As a reference, a cosmetic renovation starts from €180/m² and a full renovation from €590/m², depending on area, condition and materials. You'll find more reference prices under “Our prices”.

How long does a full renovation take?

An average flat (70–90 m²) usually takes 8 to 12 weeks, depending on scope and permits. We give you a written schedule with the quote.

Do I need a works permit?

Renovations that don't touch structure or façade usually require a minor-works notice. We help you with the necessary paperwork.

Can I live in the home during the works?

For partial renovations sometimes yes; for full ones it's best not to. We assess it at the visit.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. All our work is guaranteed, and materials carry their manufacturer's warranty.
