Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to renovate an office or unit?

A full office renovation typically ranges from ~€400/m² (basic) to ~€900/m² (premium), depending on systems and finishes. Ask us for an estimate with the calculator.

Can you work at night or on weekends?

Yes. We adapt the works schedule to reduce the impact on your activity and your neighbours'.

Do you handle the activity licence?

Yes, we coordinate the necessary works and activity licences and the technical documentation.

Do you work turnkey?

Yes. From design to handover, with a single site manager and progress reports.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes, all work is guaranteed and materials carry their manufacturer's warranty.
