What these prices include

Prices are indicative and do not constitute a contractual offer. They include labour and basic materials only where expressly stated. They do not include VAT, permits, municipal fees, technical design, special skips or structural work. After the visit, we provide a detailed quote for your approval.

  • We clearly state “supply and installation” or “installation only”.
  • For jobs priced per m², a minimum order may apply: just ask.
  • The applicable VAT is determined by the nature of the works; prices are shown excluding VAT.
  • Parking, difficult access, restricted hours and special skips are assessed after the visit.

Frequently asked questions

Do the prices include VAT?

No. The applicable rate is determined by the nature of the works and the legal conditions.

Are materials included?

Only where “supply and installation” is expressly stated.

Can the final quote vary?

Yes. It's calculated once we know the measurements, condition, materials and complexity.

Which areas do you work in?

Barcelona and towns up to roughly 50 km away, depending on the project.

Do you do commercial renovations?

Yes: offices, shops, restaurants, beauty centres and other spaces.

Do you handle licences?

It can be assessed when the project requires it; it isn't included in the indicative prices.
